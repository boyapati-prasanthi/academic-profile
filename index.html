ABOUT

Research focused on intelligent solutions for healthcare and personalized systems.

My research interests focus on medical image analysis, computer-aided diagnosis, recommender systems, and medical image segmentation. I am particularly interested in developing computational methods for accurate analysis, diagnosis, and decision-making from complex data.

My research areas include the extraction of brain tissues from high-resolution human MRI brain images, the design and development of computer-aided diagnosis systems for brain tumour diseases, personalized recommender systems with a focus on reducing sparsity and improving ranking quality, and medical image segmentation.
