CSE 303 — Machine Learning

SRM University AP · Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Course CodeCSE 303
CategoryCC
L · T · P · C3 · 0 · 1 · 4

Course objectives

  1. Introduce Machine Learning and the tasks involved in developing machine-learning applications.
  2. Understand regression, classification, and clustering algorithms.
  3. Apply algorithms to real-world problems, optimize learned models, and report expected accuracy.
  4. Understand advances in Machine Learning and research articles.

Course outcomes

Outcome 1 — Machine-learning application development→ Outcome 2 — Learning algorithms→ Outcome 3 — Data and dimensionality→ Outcome 4 — Recognition and classification→

Course units

UNIT I

Introduction, Model Evaluation & Regression

Introduction to Machine Learning, types of learning, models and learning algorithms, hypothesis space, inductive bias, training/testing/validation, cross-validation, overfitting, underfitting, linear regression, polynomial regression, and regression-fit evaluation.

UNIT II

Decision Trees, Instance-Based Learning & Feature Reduction

Decision-tree learning, ID3, numerical problems, k-nearest neighbour, curse of dimensionality, feature selection, PCA, LDA, and recommender systems.

UNIT III

Bayesian Learning & Support Vector Machines

Probability and Bayesian learning, Bayes optimal decisions, Naïve Bayes, Support Vector Machines, dual formulation, maximum-margin methods, kernel functions, and SVM exercises using Python.

UNIT IV

Artificial Neural Networks

Artificial Neural Networks, biological motivation, ANN representation, McCulloch-Pitts neuron, perceptron learning, ADALINE, MADALINE, back-propagation, multilayer networks, and Radial Basis Function Neural Networks.

UNIT V

Ensembles, Clustering & Computational Learning Theory

Bagging, boosting, Random Forest, rule-fusion techniques, k-means clustering, hierarchical clustering, and computational learning theory including sample complexity, finite hypothesis spaces, and VC dimension.

Laboratory

The syllabus includes 15 Python/ML laboratory experiments, covering Python basics, ML packages, regression, distance measures, k-NN, ID3, covariance/correlation, PCA, Naïve Bayes, SVM, perceptron, ADALINE/MADALINE, back-propagation ANN, k-means, and hierarchical clustering.
